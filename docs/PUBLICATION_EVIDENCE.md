## Why INTW uses 2026 and has no arXiv link

The original site places INTW under 2025, while its citation says 2026. The
[publisher's journal page](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0010465525004928)
and [publisher-deposited Crossref metadata](https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1016/j.cpc.2025.109991)
both identify **volume 320, March 2026, article 109991**. The local entry therefore
uses **2026** and the publisher's sentence-case title. The `2025` in the DOI is not
the journal issue year. Crossref does not supply a separate online publication date
for this record; no online-first date has been inferred.

The original INTW “arXiv” link points to
`https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2512.06176`, which is conclusively the unrelated
*Polarons from first principles* review by Dai, Lafuente-Bartolome and Giustino.
That link has been removed from INTW. Title/author searches did not locate a
verifiable INTW arXiv record; none has been invented. The published open-access DOI
remains available. If the author supplies a genuine preprint URL later, verify it
before adding it. The publisher-linked
[Mendeley software deposit](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/7fs29p69f7/1)
is a code dataset, not an arXiv substitute.

## Other decisions about authors and publication status

- EPW's arXiv v1 lists 16 authors; the journal and Crossref list 19, matching the
  current website. The final published list includes Jae-Mo Lihm, Hitoshi Mori and
  Sabyasachi Tiwari in their published positions. The journal list is retained.
- The two APS acceptance pages establish acceptance, but do not provide a final
  journal volume/article citation in the retrieved record. These entries remain
  in the preprint collection, with their acceptance explicitly displayed. They
  must not be described as unreviewed, nor as final published articles. Their
  collection year remains the initial arXiv posting year; the citation gives the
  2026 acceptance date. Recheck the DOI at launch for a version of record.
- Journal-author spelling and initials follow publisher-deposited metadata.
  Preprint authors follow the existing website and matching arXiv author order.
  For Extreme-Band-Gap, arXiv renders Jon's surname without the joining hyphen;
  the existing site's `Jon Lafuente-Bartolome` is retained as a spelling variant,
  without changing identity or author order.
- The owner supplied the Nature record for Extreme-Band-Gap. Its indexed publisher
  page was checked on 26 October 2026 and identifies a published online, accepted
  manuscript awaiting the final version of record. Its verified publication date
  supports the 2026 journal grouping; the citation explicitly says “article in
  press.” No final volume or article number is inferred. Recheck those details
  when the version of record is available.
- Source title capitalization is retained except INTW's publisher title; spaces
  and the Tl/Si(111) notation are normalized. No abstracts are copied into the
  bibliography.

## What these checks do and do not establish

Some publisher pages could not be retrieved directly because their redirects or
access controls blocked the request. The checks therefore also used publication
details supplied to Crossref, publisher pages available through search, and arXiv
records. These establish which papers the entries refer to and support their
listed details. They cannot guarantee that every visitor can open every publisher page.

All 17 papers listed on the original site were included. This does not establish
whether other papers were missing from that original list. Acceptance and
publication details may have changed since the check date; revisit the publisher
records before launch.
