I completed my BSc in Physics (2014), MSc in Nanoscience and Nanotechnology (2015), and PhD in Physics of Nanostructures and Advanced Materials (2020) at EHU. My [doctoral thesis](https://addi.ehu.es/handle/10810/50519) was supervised by Asier Eiguren and Idoia G. Gurtubay. 

From 2021 to 2023, I was a postdoctoral fellow in the [Center for Quantum Materials Engineering](https://www.cqme.org/) led by Feliciano Giustino, at the [Oden Institute for Computational Engineering and Sciences](https://oden.utexas.edu/), [The University of Texas at Austin](https://www.utexas.edu/). After a subsequent postdoctoral appointment at the [Donostia International Physics Center](https://dipc.ehu.eus/es), where I worked with María Blanco-Rey, I joined the Department of Physics at EHU as an assistant professor in September 2023.

In 2025, I received the Psi-k Volker Heine Early Career Investigator Award in recognition of my contributions to first-principles studies of electron-phonon interactions in solids.

I am currently establishing an independent research program and [building a research team](/join-us/) within the broader [Computational Theory in Condensed Matter Physics group](https://www.ehu.eus/en/web/computational-many-body-theory/home) at EHU.
