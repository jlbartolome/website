I am happy to discuss possible Bachelor and Master thesis projects, as well as shorter research projects or internships. 

Projects may start from topics covered in solid-state physics, such as electronic bands, phonons, semiconductors, magnetism, superconductivity, or defects, and can develop toward first-principles methods and materials for electronics, energy conversion, or quantum technologies.

Previous experience with ab initio calculations or high-performance computing is not required for introductory projects.
